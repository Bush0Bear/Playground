How to play

Roll six dice and click the ones that score to set them aside (you must keep at least one scoring die per roll). Keep rolling the rest to build your total, but if a roll scores nothing you Farkle and lose the whole turn — so bank before you get greedy. Set all six aside and you get hot dice: pick them all back up and keep the streak going.

Only 1s, 5s and three-of-a-kind score in this basic model. A 2, 3, 4 or 6 on its own is worth nothing.
