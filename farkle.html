The hold rule

With the hold rule on, click a die once to lock it (score it) and again to hold it — a die you keep, unscored, to build a combo later (e.g. hold two 6s hoping to roll a third for 600). Every roll must still produce a new score (a fresh 1/5 or a completed combo) or you Farkle and lose the turn. Fewer dice rerolled means more risk — that's the gamble.

Only 1s, 5s and three-of-a-kind score. For the optimal-play advisor, run python -m farkle --hold advise … from the repo.
